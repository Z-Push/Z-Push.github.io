Today we announce the release of the IMAP-Backend for Z-Push.

Using the IMAP backend with Z-Push any IMAP compatible mail server is enabled to push emails directly and in realtime to pdas and smartphones.

Have fun pushing IMAP-E-Mails to PDAs 🙂
